The six-session base that had been building above the July swing lows failed decisively today, and there is nothing in the confirmation stack that authorizes buying into it.

The session was a clean structural break, not a routine dip. That is distribution into the close, not stabilization.

Probability skew is now the most bearish of this arc, with a heavily down-first tilt and a median path to the liquidation-caution level measured in days rather than weeks. Volatility has broken above 20 and cleared its 50-day average, which explicitly reduces confidence in moving-average support, and it has done so without a 25–30 capitulation spike — meaning no washout low has been registered. Zero of five buy conditions are met against four of five defensive conditions. Patience is the position.

## Market Regime

The structural regime remains Late Bull / Topping for a seventh consecutive session, and today's action deepens rather than resolves the topping process. The four regime inputs now line up in a distinctly late-cycle configuration: extension is benign, valuation support is absent, credit is neutral rather than helpful, and breadth is at its cycle-low reference.

On extension, price has compressed to just over four percent above the 200-day — normal territory, and one of the few genuinely constructive readings in the pack. The multi-year picture remains unbroken: the three-year chart shows an intact sequence of higher highs and higher lows with price far above a steadily rising 200-day, and the 50-day is still comfortably above it. That is the bull skeleton, and it is not damaged today.

Everything that confirms a bull, however, is missing. The equity risk premium is stable but effectively nil, sitting in the valuation-ceiling band where the mandate is trim bias and no aggressive adds. Junk-versus-investment-grade spreads are essentially on their one-year average — neutral, not confirming, and notably not yet warning of systemic stress. Breadth is the weakest link: the McClellan Volume Summation Index has ground down to its cycle-low reference through the entire period price was churning near the highs, and net new 52-week highs on the NYSE have faded to barely positive. That is textbook narrow leadership.

The practical consequence is a raised bar: in this regime the probability threshold for action is 70 percent, and today's skew points the wrong way entirely.

The awkward consequence is that the re-entry floor implied by a 0.5 percent premium sits roughly one percent *above* today's close. The resolution is straightforward: multiples permit patience, the premium forbids aggression, and the premium governs re-entry.
