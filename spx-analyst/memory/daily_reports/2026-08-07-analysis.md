## Market Regime

The structural bias remains Late Bull / Topping, held for a seventh consecutive session, and today's evidence deepened rather than resolved the late-cycle profile. Extension above the 200-day has crossed into the elevated band at just over 10% — the highest reading of the arc — which mandates meaningfully reduced drift and flags elevated mean-reversion pressure. That single fact does more to define the regime than the record close does.

The four regime inputs split cleanly. Price extension is elevated. The equity risk premium is stable but compressed into the valuation-ceiling zone, offering no structural cushion. Credit is the one unambiguous positive: the junk-versus-investment-grade spread has tightened to the lowest level of the year, sitting at the floor of its multi-year range — risk appetite is confirming equity strength, not diverging from it. Breadth is the offsetting negative: the McClellan Volume Summation Index has lifted off its two-week shelf to a level last seen in June, but remains deep in the lower half of its one-year range against peaks well above 1,300, and net new 52-week highs on the NYSE sit near their cycle-low shelf, a fraction of the February peak.

That combination — elevated extension, compressed valuation cushion, confirming credit, non-confirming participation — is the textbook late-bull signature. It carries three consequences for the run: the probability bar rises to 70%, the shallow 23.6% retracement becomes a hard pause zone with no deployment permitted, and bullish action requires a materially higher standard of proof than it would in a mid-cycle tape. Nothing today met that standard.

## Evidence and Tensions

Five tensions are live, and today's posture resolves each in favor of patience.

**Breadth versus record price.** The bullish read is that the McClellan Volume Summation has ticked up to its best reading since June, off the two-week shelf, suggesting participation may finally be broadening. The bearish read is that it remains deep in the lower half of its one-year range with peaks far above, while net new 52-week highs sit near the cycle-low shelf and a small fraction of the February peak. *Resolution:* one tick up does not undo weeks of narrowing. All-time highs on this level of participation is narrow leadership and a topping warning — caution stays elevated.

**Money flow versus the price high.** Bullishly, five-day money flow has recovered off the buy-watch low as price broke to a new closing high, and the one-month and three-month readings remain healthy and clear of the trim line. Bearishly, money flow peaked far higher on August 4 and now reads dramatically lower with price *higher* — a multi-session bearish divergence at record price, with daily RSI likewise well below its August 4 print. *Resolution:* longer-timeframe non-confirmation outweighs a one-day rebound. The divergence is intact.

**Valuation ceiling versus record price.** Bullishly, forward estimates were revised up and the 10-year eased, holding the multiple inside the band where confluence buying is permitted, with the premium stable rather than contracting. Bearishly, the premium sits in the ceiling band where no aggressive adds are allowed, the trailing multiple is in the mid-24s, and the valuation-confirmed floor is roughly five percent below spot. *Resolution:* trim bias; no valuation support exists near current price.

**Probability clears 65% but fails the regime bar.** Bullishly, the adjusted up-first reading clears the Early/Mid Bull threshold for the first time in this arc, with a two-session median to the upside target and a strong breakout cascade. Bearishly, the governing regime requires 70%, the exhaustion discount is already applied, and the distribution is labeled balanced. *Resolution:* probability is never used in isolation and fails the applicable threshold — not actionable.

**Complacency versus extension.** Bullishly, a sub-15 VIX beneath its 50-day and a falling put/call describe an orderly, low-stress tape supportive of drift and reliable moving-average support. Bearishly, that same configuration into double-digit extension above the 200-day is the classic trim warning, and the absence of any volatility spike in this entire advance means there is no capitulative anchor for re-entry and maximum unhedged exposure into any decline. *Resolution:* treat complacency as a risk input, not a comfort.

The unifying tension is that the strongest bullish evidence set of the entire arc — record close, top-third finish, tightest credit of the year, best probability reading yet — still fails to clear the bar that a late-cycle regime imposes. That is not a reason to fight the trend; it is a reason to sell into it rather than buy it.
