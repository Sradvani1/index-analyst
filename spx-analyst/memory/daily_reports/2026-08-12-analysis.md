## Today's Posture

Trim bias and patience. Hold core exposure, maintain the trim ladder into the 7,758 to 7,794 record zone, and deploy no new capital at spot. Nothing in today's session changed the governing conclusion: the setup is not actionable long.

The session was constructive at the margin. Price closed at 7,748.50, up 0.26%, ending three consecutive lower closes and reclaiming a flattening 20-day SMA after Tuesday's marginal breach. That is a genuine short-term structural improvement, and it pulls the tape back into the upper half of the 7,700 to 7,794 shelf. But it is the seventh consecutive session in which the August 5 high at 7,793.68 has failed to confirm, and price has not made a higher high in five sessions.

The reason this remains a hold-and-monitor rather than an add is the confluence of restrictive conditions on the other side: participation at record price is the weakest of the year, money flow sits far below its August 4 peak at a comparable price level, valuation offers essentially no cushion over the 10-year, and the probability edge fails even the lower 65% bar, let alone the 70% threshold this regime demands. Signal alignment stands at three trim conditions met against one buy condition. That is not a green light; it is a reason to stay disciplined and let the shelf resolve.
