The bullish case is real but incomplete. Under an Early or Mid Bull regime that probability would be actionable. It is not, because the governing regime is Late Bull / Topping and the required bar is 70% — failed now for a thirteenth consecutive session.

Against that, the internals continue to narrow. Net new 52-week highs have decayed to the weakest reading of the whole advance while the index sits within half a percent of its all-time high; five-day money flow has fallen sharply from its early-August peak against higher price; and the session closed in the bottom third of its range after fading from the intraday high. Extension remains elevated versus the 200-day with rally exhaustion at Moderate, and the valuation cushion is pinned at the ceiling.

Signal alignment is four trim conditions against one buy condition. Hold the core, trim into strength inside the shelf, deploy no new capital, and let the market either confirm the high with breadth or reset to the fib band below.

## Market Regime

The structural bias remains Late Bull / Topping, unchanged for a sixth consecutive session, and every classification input points the same way. Price extension sits near +9.9% above the 200-day — right at the boundary between moderate and elevated — after touching just over 10% last session. That is the top of the range for this advance and the level at which mean-reversion pressure begins to compete meaningfully with drift.

The equity risk premium is stable but compressed into the valuation-ceiling band, offering essentially no cushion versus risk-free yield. Stable is not deteriorating, but at this level "stable" simply means the ceiling is holding rather than lifting.

Credit is the one unambiguous positive. The junk-versus-investment-grade spread has compressed to its tightest of the year on a clean multi-week slope, and risk appetite in the corporate bond market is fully confirming equity strength. That single confirmation is why this is a topping-risk regime rather than a deteriorating one.

Breadth is where the classification is decided. Participation is not expanding into the record; it is contracting.

Elevated extension, a ceiling-level risk premium, confirming credit, and narrowing breadth is the textbook Late Bull / Topping combination. The 70% Monte Carlo threshold and hard-pause treatment of the shallow retracement zone follow directly from it.
