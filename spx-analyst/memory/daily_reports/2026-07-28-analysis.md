Under a Late Bull / Topping regime the bar for bullish action is deliberately higher, and one day of improvement inside a five-day range does not clear it.

Next session, the posture only changes on evidence: a decisive close back above the 50-day with breadth turning up, or a flush to the liquidation-caution shelf accompanied by a volatility spike and reversal.

The asymmetry in timing reinforces this. Median time to the downside reference is a fraction of the median time to the swing-high upside reference. In other words, if the market is going to touch one of the two levels, the odds and the clock both favor the lower one.

The cascade structure is the reason patience beats bargain-hunting here. That is why this is defensive patience rather than defense — the structural drift still points up. But drift is a slow-moving expectation, while the first-hit probabilities describe the next few sessions, and those favor a lower test first. Cash drag risk is modest and acceptable given the skew.
