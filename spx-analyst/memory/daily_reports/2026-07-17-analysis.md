Defensive / patience. Today's session broke the multi-week stalemate to the downside, and the posture shifts firmly toward capital protection with no adds. The break is not just a price event — it is confirmed by the internals. But the combination of a lost SMA cluster, a down-first edge, and a zero-premium valuation warrants defense over dip-buying. Hold existing exposure, protect capital, and wait for the fib/ERP confluence just below to be tested and confirmed before considering any partial re-entry.

## Market Regime

The market remains in a Late Bull / Topping regime — a structurally intact but maturing uptrend now showing its first clean crack in weeks. The long-term architecture is still bullish: the 50-day sits above a rising 200-day, and price holds roughly 6.7% above the 200-day, moderate extension that is nowhere near the extreme readings that typically mark a blow-off. The Rally Exhaustion Score is Low and full drift still applies, meaning the correction has room to resolve as a normal pullback rather than a topping unwind.

What keeps this firmly in the late-cycle bucket is the persistent refusal of the confirming layers to validate the recovery:

- **Breadth is narrow and near its lows.** The McClellan Volume Summation Index sits around 900 — pinned near the bottom of its year-long range — and net new 52-week highs on the NYSE are barely positive around 2.0. Neither turned up to support the multi-week grind at the swing high; leadership stayed thin throughout.
- **Credit is neutral, not confirming.** The junk bond yield spread near 1.31 hovers around its recent average, offering no risk-appetite tailwind and drifting slightly wider off its lows.
- **Valuation is at a hard ceiling.** With the equity risk premium at essentially zero, equities offer no meaningful premium over risk-free yield — a classic late-cycle constraint on aggressive adds.

The regime call is unchanged from prior sessions, but the character has shifted: for the first time in the arc, price action has joined the internals in signaling caution rather than fighting them.
