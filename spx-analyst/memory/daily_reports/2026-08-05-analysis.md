The four structural inputs split the way late-cycle tapes usually do. Price extension is stretched. Credit is unambiguously supportive: the junk-versus-investment-grade spread has tightened to the tightest reading of the trailing year and sits below its reference line, showing no risk-appetite deterioration behind the record price. The consequence is procedural: the action threshold for probability-based deployment stays at the elevated Late Bull bar, and the shallow 23.6% retracement is a hard pause zone with no deployment permitted. In this regime, bullish action requires a higher bar, and today's evidence does not clear it.
