The reason is that price strength is running well ahead of the internal and valuation backdrop that should confirm it. The up-first edge, while a real reversal, still fails every regime threshold.

For the next session, the operating stance is to lighten into continued strength as price grinds toward the swing high, and to stay patient rather than add. This is not a defensive crouch — the trend skeleton is intact and healthy — but a Late Bull / Topping regime near resistance with non-confirming internals does not clear the bar for fresh deployment. Let strength do the selling.

## Market Regime

The structural regime remains Late Bull / Topping, an intact but maturing bull colliding with late-cycle warning signs. The mechanical skeleton is healthy: the 50-day (7,403) sits comfortably above a rising 200-day (6,948), price is +8.5% above the 200-day, and the 125-day moving average (7,061) is trending steadily higher well below spot, confirming the longer uptrend has not broken. This is not a market in structural distress.

But three of the four regime inputs argue for elevated caution rather than aggression. First, extension: +8.5% above the 200-day sits in the moderate-extension band — not extreme, but enough to temper drift expectations. Second, credit: the junk-versus-investment-grade spread at 1.34% remains elevated and near recent highs rather than tightening, so credit is not confirming equity strength. Third, breadth: the McClellan Volume Summation Index around 933 sits below the "strong" threshold, and net new 52-week highs on the NYSE at just +2.38% remain muted against a price recovery that has carried the index back toward its highs. Breadth is participating grudgingly, not broadly.

The one input that has improved is the valuation/sentiment complex at the margin — the equity risk premium held stable and Fear & Greed lifted off deep-fear lows — but the ERP at 0.48% remains inside the valuation-ceiling band. Taken together, a healthy trend and moderate extension keep this a bull, but non-confirming breadth and credit, plus a wafer-thin valuation cushion, keep it firmly in the topping phase. That combination sets a high bar for bullish action and favors trimming into strength.

## Evidence and Tensions

The posture rests on a clean central tension: an intact and now momentum-improving bull skeleton is grinding into resistance while the internals and valuation that should confirm the move refuse to do so. That is why trimming into strength — not chasing the breakout, and not defending against a break that hasn't occurred — is the justified stance.

**Valuation ceiling versus the breakout.** The bullish read is that price cleared the twice-rejected 23.6% fib and reclaimed the ERP floor, signaling renewed upside momentum. The bearish read is that the ERP at 0.48% is pinned at the valuation ceiling, where the framework calls for trim bias and no aggressive adds — the cushion is essentially zero precisely at resistance. Today's posture resolves this in favor of the valuation constraint: a breakout that erodes an already-zero cushion is an opportunity to lighten, not to add.

**Breadth and credit versus the rally.** The bullish read is that price has recovered strongly toward the swing high, suggesting the pullback is over. The bearish read is that the McClellan index (~933, below strong) and net new highs (+2.38%) remain muted while the junk spread (1.34%) stays elevated — internals and credit are not confirming the move, the signature of narrow leadership into resistance. The posture treats this non-confirmation as decisive: price near highs without broadening participation warrants elevated caution, not deployment.

**Monte Carlo flip versus the threshold.** The bullish read is that the simulation flipped to 60.6% up-first with a rising drift path — a real momentum improvement. The bearish read is that 60.6% fails every regime threshold and the setup is therefore not actionable, with a fast 2-day path up leading directly into a resistance zone. The resolution: the flip confirms momentum has turned but does not, on its own, authorize action; it is read through the regime, not in isolation.

Confirming evidence — the cleared 23.6% fib, the up-first flip, the benign sub-16 VIX, the intact 50>200 trend, and recovering sentiment — is real and keeps this a bull rather than a breakdown. But none of it overrides a zero-cushion ERP and non-confirming breadth into resistance. Trim into strength, and wait for a confirmed pullback before re-engaging.
