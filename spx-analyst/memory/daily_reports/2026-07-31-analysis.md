Patience costs little when the probability edge is absent.

Any deployment at the 7,467–7,479 shelf is therefore a tactical trade against a still-restrictive valuation backdrop and must be sized accordingly. Full-conviction, valuation-blessed buying requires materially lower prices, a lower 10-year, or upward earnings revisions.
